Pendulum sonification

One swing, one note. The rod’s length is chosen from the note; the note is read back out of the motion.

running silently

An ideal, undamped pendulum. Rod lengths run 8.4–33.6 cm, giving swings of 0.859–1.719 Hz, resampled 256× so that a 23.4 cm rod sounds as C4. The strip below the panels is the score: sixteen notes, 13.54 s, resolving onto its own first note.

What this does and does not claim
  • Damping is not modelled. A pendulum that swings forever has no energy source; real ones decay, which is why clock pendulums need an escapement. This is an ideal oscillator, shown for clarity.
  • The audio is a resampling, not a recording. ×256 is sample-rate change. What you hear is a sonification of sampled motion.
  • Small-angle approximation. Amplitude is 0.36 rad (about 20.6°). Beyond roughly 0.4 rad the true period lengthens and a pure sinusoid stops describing the swing.
  • The apparatus is not built. Rod lengths are those a physical build would require.
